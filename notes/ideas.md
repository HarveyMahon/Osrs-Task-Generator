# Parked ideas

Things we considered and decided not to build. Nothing here is live on the site.

## Combo challenges (parked 2026-11-09)

Chain two or three short tasks into one challenge, for example "10 Vorkath kills, then a hard clue, then 5 Hallowed Sepulchre runs".

- **Why it was suggested**: multiplies variety without any new data, since every existing short challenge becomes a building block.
- **Why it is parked**: judged unlikely to be fun. A chain of unrelated tasks feels like a to-do list rather than a challenge.
- **If we reconsider**: build chains only from Casual-length pieces so the total stays inside a tier window, keep each chain to one theme (all PvM, all skilling) rather than mixing, and reuse the "boss tour" format in `data/monsters.json` (`feats`), which is already a hand-written themed chain.

## Installable offline app (declined 2026-11-09)

A web app manifest and service worker. Declined: playing OSRS needs a connection anyway, so offline use adds nothing.
